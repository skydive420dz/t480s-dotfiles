* Storage Constraint
The only detected internal target is one NVMe device.  Same-disk Btrfs
snapshots are useful recovery points but are not backups.  The T430 Btrfs send
design stays deferred until a real independent destination is selected.

The proposed installation basis is GPT, a 1 GiB EFI System Partition, about
32 GiB dedicated swap for hibernation margin, and Btrfs for the remaining
space.  Exact devices, boundaries, UUIDs, subvolumes, mounts, and resume identity
must be reviewed at the destructive storage gate.
